CineView v2.0.1

我写的落地页框架。页面就是普通 HTML 加几个自定义元素：分成几幕，每幕一条时间线，读者往下滚，时间线就往前走。没有运行时依赖，还附带一套给 AI 用的设计技能，让它先和你定好分镜，再动手写页面。 A landing-page framework I wrote. Pages are plain HTML plus a few custom elements: split into scenes, each with a timeline that moves as the reader scrolls. No runtime dependencies, and a design skill that has an AI plan the storyboard with you before it writes the page.
